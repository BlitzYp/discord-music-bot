A QUICK MILESTONE 2 TEST RUN
----------------------------
1. Join a voice channel. /play Good Lookin', then queue Like Whiskey, F150, Kinda Crazy and Dreams.
2. /queue to see the numbers. /move from:4 to:1, then /queue again.
3. /remove position:2, then /shuffle, checking /queue after each.
4. /pause, wait a few seconds, /resume. Then /pause and /skip: the next song should start playing, not stay paused.
5. /volume percent:50, then /volume with no number to see it.
6. /stop. /play the 19-second video, then /loop mode:track and let it end: it should start again.
7. Queue two or three more songs, /loop mode:queue, and /skip a few times: skipped songs go to the end of
   /queue instead of disappearing.
8. Leave the voice channel and wait 60 seconds: the bot should leave too.
